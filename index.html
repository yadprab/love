Dear reader,

I'm Yathavan. Thirty-one, Chennai, senior software developer — which mostly means I argue with computers by day and think about dinner the rest of the time.

Cinema got there first, though. Before anything else, that was the love story, and it never really ended. My Letterboxd is the most honest profile I own — go read that before you finish this.

I eat non-veg, happily and without ceremony. Chinese food and Kerala food are where I'm most myself. I'm an atheist, quietly rather than loudly.

And I'm here for one thing, plainly: to start the love chapter. That's the whole ambition.

What I value

  • Honesty, said out loud Say the thing. I'd rather hear it plainly and early than work it out from clues.
  • Staying in the difficult conversation Anyone can talk when it's easy. What I care about is how we do it when it isn't.
  • A working inner child Bring the shenanigans — the real, silly, unguarded kind. Not the permanently bubbly performance of it.
  • Having a politics You don't have to arrive at my conclusions, but you do have to have thought about it. "I don't follow politics" is its own answer.

My non-negotiables

  • My plate is my own I eat non-veg, and not on a schedule. If certain days are meant to be meat-free, that won't be my house.
  • Faith is fine. Fate isn't. Believe in god — genuinely, no argument from me. But daily rituals, and life decisions taken on astrology, are not something I can live inside.
  • Queerness isn't up for debate Queer people are people, fully and without an asterisk. If that's a position you'd argue with, we should stop here.

If we had a first date

Ustad Hotel on ECR, Kerala food, nowhere to be afterwards. Or a film — your pick, and I'll sit through almost anything once. Or the simplest version, which is probably the best one: the beach, ice cream, fried fish, and a conversation that takes as long as it takes.

Until the beach, then,

Yathavan · Chennai
