## The letter

*Hello, then,*

I'm Yathavan. Thirty-one, Chennai, senior software developer. That mostly means I argue with computers by day and think about dinner the rest of the time.

Cinema was the first love, and I have never quite got over it. My Letterboxd is the most honest profile I own. Read it after this, and judge me freely.

I eat non-veg, happily and without ceremony. Chinese food and Kerala food are where I'm most myself. I'm an atheist, not the sort who brings it up at dinner. You'd have found out eventually.

And I'm here for one thing, plainly: to start the love chapter. That's the whole ambition.

### What I value

- **Honesty, said out loud** — Say the thing. I'd rather hear it plainly and early than work it out from clues.
- **Staying in the difficult conversation** — Anyone can talk when it's easy. What I care about is how we do it when it isn't.
- **A working inner child** — Bring the shenanigans. I mean the real, silly, unguarded kind, not the permanently bubbly performance of it.
- **You have political opinions** — I want someone who has thought about how things are run and who they actually work for. We don't have to agree on everything, but if politics is something you've never bothered with, we probably won't fit.

### A few things that don't move

I eat meat, and not on a calendar. No days off, nothing arranged around an almanac.

You can believe in god. Genuinely, no argument from me. What I can't do is run a life on star charts and auspicious dates.

And queer people are people, fully and without an asterisk. That one isn't a preference, it's just where I stand.

That's the whole list. Everything else is negotiable, and I'm better company than that paragraph makes me sound.

### If we had a first date

Ustad Hotel on ECR, Kerala food, and nowhere to be afterwards. Or a film. Your pick, I'll sit through almost anything once. Or the simplest version, which is probably the best one: the beach, ice cream, fried fish, and finding out whether we run out of things to say.

### Sign-off

*Until the beach, then,*
Yathavan · Chennai
